Mongolian kinship vocabulary is precise: the **father's side (avga)** and **mother's side (nagats)**, **older and younger siblings**, and **relatives by marriage** all have distinct names. Below are the most common modern terms. The app's **Kinship** tool applies these rules to your own tree automatically.

## Siblings

| Who | Term |
|---|---|
| Older brother | **Akh** |
| Older sister | **Egch** |
| Younger sibling | **Düü** |
| Same father, different mother | **Etseg negt** akh / egch / düü |
| Same mother, different father | **Ekh negt** akh / egch / düü |
| Step-parent's child | **Dagavar** akh / egch / düü |

## Father's side — avga

| Who | Term |
|---|---|
| Father's brother | **Avga akh** (*ikh avga* if older than father, *baga avga* if younger) |
| Father's sister | **Avga egch** |
| Wife of father's brother | **Avga bergen** |
| Father's parents | **Övöö, emee** |

## Mother's side — nagats

| Who | Term |
|---|---|
| Mother's brother | **Nagats akh** |
| Mother's sister | **Nagats egch** |
| Husband of mother's sister | **Nagats khürgen akh** |
| Mother's parents | **Nagats övöö, nagats emee** |

## Cousins, grandchildren, nephews and nieces

| Who | Term |
|---|---|
| Child of father's brother | **Üyeel** |
| Child of mother's sister | **Bül** |
| Child of father's sister / mother's brother | **Naga** |
| Second cousin (shared great-grandparent) | **Khayaal** |
| Son's child | **Ach** |
| Daughter's child | **Zee** |
| Brother's child | **Ach** |
| Sister's child | **Zee** |

## Relatives by marriage

| Who | Term |
|---|---|
| Son's wife | **Ber** |
| Daughter's husband | **Khürgen** |
| Older brother's wife | **Bergen** |
| Younger brother's wife | **Düü ber** |
| Older sister's husband | **Khürgen akh** |
| Spouse's parents | **Khadam etseg, khadam ekh** |
| Spouse's siblings | **Khadam akh / egch / düü** |
| Parents of your child's spouse | **Khud** (men), **khudgui** (women) |
| Husbands of two sisters (to each other) | **Baz** |
| Stepfather, stepmother | **Khoid etseg, khoid ekh** |
| Spouse's child from an earlier union | **Dagavar khüü / okhin** |

> Usage varies by region and even by family — especially *ach / zee* and *bül / naga*. Ask your elders what your family says.
